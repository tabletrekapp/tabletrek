Table Trek: privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

Table Trek does not collect any information. Everything stays on the iPhone or iPad it is used on.

What the app keeps, and where

  • The player's name, if one is typed in. It is only used to say hello in the app and on the progress report. It can be changed or removed at any time in Settings (the gear) → Name.
  • Progress: answers, stars, rewards and settings.

All of this is stored on the device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not use the internet.

Reports and backups

A grown-up can choose to share a progress report or save a backup file from Settings. These use the iPhone or iPad's own share sheet, and go only where the grown-up sends them (for example Messages, Mail or the Files app).

Deleting everything

In the app, Settings → Erase everything removes the name and all progress. Deleting the app from the device does the same. A backup file that a grown-up saved somewhere stays wherever it was saved until it is deleted there.

Children

Table Trek is made for children to practise times tables and division. Because it collects no information at all, nothing about a child ever leaves the device.
