Table Trek: support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com and you'll get a reply, usually within a few days.

Common questions

How do I keep progress safe, or move it to another device?
Tap the gear, then Save a backup, and save the file (for example to Files or iCloud Drive). On the other device, tap the gear, then Restore from a backup. An iPhone and an iPad keep separate progress until you do this.
How do I change which tables come up?
Tap the gear, then Tables. Learn a table, on the home page, teaches a new one.
What is the typing check?
Answers are timed, so before the first round a one-minute check measures how fast the player types numbers. Slow typing is then never mistaken for slow thinking. A reminder suggests doing it again once a month.
Why are the games locked?
The games are a reward for practice. The Arcade and Face maker open after 2 minutes of answering each day. Fruit slash time is earned 5 minutes at a time by practising. A badge on each game shows how long is left.
Where can a grown-up see progress?
Tap the gear, then Grown-ups. It shows what is solid, where the slips are, and a report you can share.
How do I start again?
Tap the gear. Reset the tables grid clears the squares and keeps the stars and rewards. Erase everything removes the name and all progress. Both ask first.
Does the app collect any data?
No. Everything stays on the device. See the privacy policy.
