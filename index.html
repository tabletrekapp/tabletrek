Table Trek

Times tables and dividing, for iPhone and iPad

Table Trek makes times tables stick. Short rounds of questions, stars for quick answers, and a grid where every fact from 2 × 2 to 12 × 12 has its own square. Get quicker and a square turns green, then gold. Still lightning fast a week later? Purple: solid.

  • Each fact is practised 3 ways: 7 × 8, 56 ÷ 8 and ? × 8 = 56.
  • Learn a new table in 4 short steps, and catch Kal the calculator's silly slips.
  • Stars buy laser wands, world food trips, penny sweets and more. The games open as a reward for practice.
  • For grown-ups: what's solid, where the slips are, and a report to share.

No adverts, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and nothing collected. It works offline.
